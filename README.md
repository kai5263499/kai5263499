# Hi, I’m Wes.

> An archaeological record of bad ideas that seemed reasonable at the time.

I’m a security engineer who likes taking things apart, figuring out how they work, and occasionally putting them back together with more dependencies.

Over 20+ years, that curiosity has wandered through cybersecurity, malware analysis, distributed systems, infrastructure, AI, and software for problems that probably could have been handled with a spreadsheet.

There is a theme here. It’s curiosity. There is not a roadmap.

## Current rabbit holes

- **AI security:** Model integrity, prompt injection, and testing what happens when the reassuring demo meets the actual internet.
- **Local AI and agent systems:** Open-weight models, private inference, and getting multiple agents to do useful work without inventing another management layer.
- **Open research and education:** Helping people understand how AI actually works, what it can do, and when to stop asking the chatbot and ask a person.

I’m building at **[Mecha Minds](https://mechaminds.ai/)**, focused on AI infrastructure, automation, and security, and **[Tensor Lab](https://tensorlab.org/)**, a nonprofit supporting open research and public-benefit education.

My commitment to local computing has escalated to an eight-GPU server. I can now complain about the cloud entirely on-premises.
